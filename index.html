    How to use PrioStorm

    • Add an idea: type and press Enter or click Add.
    • ⚙ button on each card expands three priority sliders:
      • Impact: how big is the positive effect? (0–5)
      • Urgency: how soon must it be done? (0–5)
      • Effort: how much work is required? (0–5, higher = more work)
    • Score = (Impact × Urgency) ÷ (Effort + 1). The badge color reflects the score — grey to teal as priority rises.
    • Edit an item: double-click its title to rename it.
    • 📝 button adds a private note to any card.
    • ⚙ Defaults sets default I/U/E values for your next item. Preset buttons (Quick Win, Big Bet…) fill these in one click.
    • ⊞ Matrix opens the Effort × Impact visualization. Urgency colors items: green = low, orange = medium, red = high.
    • Drag cards to re-order (with Auto-sort off) or let the list sort itself by score.
    • Work is auto-saved in your browser. Use multiple projects to keep ideas separate.
    • Copy exports ranked text. CSV downloads a spreadsheet including notes.
    • Press Esc to close this guide.
