    How to use PrioStorm

    • Add an idea: type a short description and press Enter or click Add.
    • Set its factors:
      • Impact (I): how big is the positive effect? (0–5)
      • Urgency (U): how soon must it be done? (0–5)
      • Effort (E): how much work is required? (0–5, higher = more work)
    • Priority score = (I × U) ÷ (E + 1). Higher is better.
    • Edit an item: double-click its title to rename it.
    • Preset shortcuts let you quickly set I/U/E defaults for your next item — e.g. "Quick Win" sets I=5 U=5 E=1.
    • Matrix quick guide:
      • Low-effort + High-impact: do these first (quick wins).
      • Low-effort + Low-impact: fit in when time allows.
      • High-effort + High-impact: plan & resource carefully.
      • High-effort + Low-impact: reconsider or drop.
    • Urgency colours items in the matrix: green = low, orange = medium, red = high.
    • Drag cards to re-order (auto-sort off) or let the list sort itself by score.
    • Your work is saved automatically in this browser — reload and it comes back.
    • Copy exports the ranked list as plain text. CSV downloads a spreadsheet.
    • Clear removes everything after confirmation.
    • Press Esc to close this guide.
